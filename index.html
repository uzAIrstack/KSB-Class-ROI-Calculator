How is this calculated?

  1. Student tuition is estimated. Undergraduate tuition is allocated using each student's total semester credits. Graduate and non-degree tuition use the applicable per-credit rate.
  2. Non-AU students generate $0 revenue. Their potential KSB share appears as Non-AU Revenue Lost instead.
  3. The course revenue share is applied. Standard and eligible CORE courses assign 100% to KSB; cross-listed courses assign 50%.
  4. The RCM share is removed. The selected 50% or 40% university share is deducted from Total Revenue to produce KSB Expense Authority.
  5. Class expenses are deducted. Eligible graduate scholarships, faculty teaching cost, Teaching Assistant cost, and added expenses are subtracted from Expense Authority.
  6. The remainder is the estimated contribution. A positive result is Net Income; a negative result is a Loss.

Undergraduate allocation

Below 12 credits, the class is priced per credit. From 12–17.99 credits, semester tuition is divided by total credits and multiplied by class credits. At 18 credits, the 18-credit semester rate is allocated proportionally. Above 18 credits, the per-credit overload is added before proportional allocation.
